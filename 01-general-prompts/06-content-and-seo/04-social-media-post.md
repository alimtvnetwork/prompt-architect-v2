# Social Media Post Generation

## Inputs
- Topic / Feature
- Target Audience
- Platform (Twitter, LinkedIn, Facebook, etc.)
- Tone (Professional, Casual, Humorous, etc.)

## 1. Structure
- Hook: A catchy opening sentence to grab attention.
- Body: 2-3 sentences explaining the value proposition or feature.
- Call to Action (CTA): Clear instruction on what the user should do next (e.g., "Read more here", "Try it out today").

## 2. Formatting
- Use appropriate emojis sparingly.
- Keep the length tailored to the platform (short for Twitter, longer for LinkedIn).
- Include 3-5 relevant hashtags at the end.

## 3. Review
- Ensure the tone matches the requested input.
- Proofread for grammar and clarity.

## Must Follow
Do not generate generic marketing fluff. Keep it punchy, relevant to developers/tech audience, and directly address the value proposition.
