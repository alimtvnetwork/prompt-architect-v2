# Instruction (must follow): SEO Optimization Instruction

When tasked with SEO optimization for a page or component, enforce the following:

## 1. Meta Tags

- Ensure `<title>` and `<meta name="description">` are present, concise, and keyword-rich.
- Include open graph (OG) tags and Twitter cards where applicable.

## 2. Semantic HTML

- Ensure correct use of heading tags (`<h1>`, `<h2>`, etc.). There should be exactly one `<h1>` per page.
- Use semantic tags like `<article>`, `<section>`, `<nav>`, `<aside>`, and `<main>`.

## 3. Image Optimization

- Ensure every `<img>` has a descriptive `alt` attribute.
- Ensure images are optimized and properly sized (use `width` and `height` attributes).

## 4. Accessibility & Performance

- Ensure ARIA labels are used for interactive elements.
- Avoid unnecessary render blocking scripts or large assets.

## 5. Report

- After optimization, provide a summary report of the SEO improvements made.

## Must Follow

Do not add marketing jargon or filler. Focus strictly on technical SEO improvements and semantic HTML.
